Our Story

A Legacy of Culinary Excellence

Founded in 2024, St. Clair Inn was born from a passion for creating extraordinary dining experiences. Our name, inspired by the Swedish word for "refined," reflects our commitment to perfection in every detail.

From the moment you step through our doors, you're embraced by an atmosphere of warmth and sophistication. Our team of world-class chefs crafts each dish with precision, using only the finest seasonal ingredients sourced from local farms and trusted suppliers across the region.

We believe that dining is more than just food - it's an art form, a celebration of flavors, and a journey that brings people together. Every plate that leaves our kitchen tells a story, and every guest becomes part of our family.
